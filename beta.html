Pinly Beta

How it works

  1. Leave the address of your Google account — the one you use on your Android phone.
  2. We add you to the tester list on Google Play.
  3. You get an email with a link to install Pinly from the Play Store.
  4. You use the app and tell us what breaks or what could be better.

It has to be the Google account you use on your phone — otherwise Google Play won't let you into the test.

Your address is used only to add you to the test and to contact you about it. It is never used for advertising, and we delete it once the test ends. Contact: pinlybiznes@gmail.com • Privacy • Terms • Home
